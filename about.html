About me

I’ve always been drawn to the space where people and systems meet.

Kelsi GoodenMBA · Learning Programs

I believe the best learning experiences do more than share information: they help people understand what to do, practice it with confidence, and carry it into their work.

My career began in education, where I spent six years translating complex information into approachable learning, coordinating programs, communicating across stakeholder groups, and adapting to different needs in fast-moving environments. That work built a strong foundation in facilitation, learner support, planning, and follow-through.

After earning my MBA, I began connecting that foundation to workplace learning, enablement, and program operations. I designed AI at Work, an interactive microlearning experience with measurable objectives, branching scenarios, practice, feedback, and assessment. I also completed an applied artificial intelligence and machine-learning program through The University of Texas at Austin, using data and predictive methods to approach practical business problems.

What ties my work together is a focus on people and performance. Whether I’m designing a course, coordinating a program, or improving a process, I want the result to be clear, useful, measurable, and easier for people to carry forward.

Outside of work, I’m drawn to travel, design, music, and places with creative energy. My year studying abroad in Paris shaped the way I see the world—and Southern California is where I hope to build my next chapter.

How I work

Curious. Organized. Grounded.

Start with the performance need.

Before building, I ask what people need to understand or do differently—and what evidence would show progress.

Make ownership visible.

Clear roles, decision points, and next steps create accountability without unnecessary complexity.

Design for the learner.

Learning works best when it is relevant, accessible, active, and connected to a real decision or task.

Close the loop.

I believe follow-through, communication, and reflection are what turn good intentions into results.
