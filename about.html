About me

I’ve always been drawn to the space where people and systems meet.

Kelsi GoodenMBA · Project Coordination

I bring structure to busy environments: clear priorities, useful documentation, and communication that helps people know what comes next.

During six years as a Language Coordinator at Ector County Independent School District (ECISD), I planned learning activities, communicated expectations, maintained documentation, and adapted delivery to different needs. Those responsibilities developed transferable habits in organization, stakeholder communication, and follow-through.

My MBA adds a business perspective to that foundation, strengthening how I approach priorities, processes, and organizational goals. I’m building on my language coordination experience to support clear project plans, effective communication, and consistent delivery.

I’m now pursuing entry-level project management roles. My portfolio connects that background to project work through workflow design, prioritization, role clarity, tracking, and digital delivery. The case studies are portfolio simulations; proposed rollout plans are clearly separated from completed deliverables and measured outcomes.

Outside of work, I’m drawn to travel, design, music, and places with creative energy. My year studying abroad in Paris shaped the way I see the world—and Southern California is where I hope to build my next chapter.

How I work

Curious. Organized. Grounded.

Clarify the objective.

Before starting, I ask what needs to change, what the deliverable is, and how stakeholders will decide whether it is ready.

Make ownership visible.

Clear roles, decision points, and next steps create accountability without unnecessary complexity.

Surface risks early.

I look for unclear requirements, missed handoffs, and competing priorities so the next decision can be made with useful context.

Close the loop.

I believe follow-through, communication, and reflection are what turn good intentions into results.
