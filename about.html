About me

I’ve always been drawn to the space where people and systems meet.

Kelsi GoodenMBA · Operations

I’m an operations-minded problem solver who believes the best systems make work clearer for the people inside them—and experiences better for the people they serve.

My career began in education, where I spent six years coordinating programs, communicating across stakeholder groups, managing competing priorities, and creating reliable experiences in fast-moving environments. That work sharpened the skills at the center of strong operations: planning, adaptability, service, and follow-through.

After earning my MBA, I began translating that foundation into corporate operations, customer success, and program work. I’m especially energized by opportunities to improve a process, connect cross-functional teams, and move a complex initiative from idea to execution.

Outside of work, I’m drawn to travel, design, music, and places with creative energy. My year studying abroad in Paris shaped the way I see the world—and Southern California is where I hope to build my next chapter.
